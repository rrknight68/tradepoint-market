Section 3 — National Tailwind

The AI Economy Needs Physical Space

The most important economic shift of our generation is creating enormous, overlooked demand for exactly the space Trade Point provides.

The Skilled Trades Supercycle
AI is the largest infrastructure buildout in American history. Google committed $10M to train electricians. IBEW recruiting 100,000+ for AI data center projects. That trades growth expands the owner-user demand pool behind Trade Point’s unit absorption.
A New Generation of Small Business Owners
Vocational enrollment rose 16% in 2025. Gen Z is entering the trades. As workers establish themselves in Cape Coral, they start their own shops — and need their own space.
Small-Bay: The Only Segment Not Overbuilding
Big-box vacancies rising. Small-bay under 50K SF at record-low vacancy nationally. The shortage is structural, not cyclical.
AI doesn't just live in the cloud — it lives in the hands of the electricians and contractors building it. Those workers need somewhere to operate. That somewhere is Trade Point.
